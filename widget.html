Frequently asked questions

Approved refunds are processed immediately and typically reach your bank account within 3–5 business days. UPI refunds are usually faster (within 24 hours). You'll receive an email confirmation once the refund is initiated.
You can cancel an order as long as it hasn't been picked up by the delivery partner. Once it's out for delivery, cancellation may not be possible, but you can still raise a complaint after delivery. A full refund is issued for all cancelled orders.
Delay under 10 min: no compensation. 10–30 min: LOW tier (coupon or credit). 30–60 min: MEDIUM tier (partial refund). Over 60 min: HIGH tier (full refund). Our AI checks order timestamps automatically — no calculation needed on your end.
Refunds may be declined if the order was already refunded, the claim doesn't match order details, or our system flags unusual activity. Declined cases are automatically escalated to our human review team, who will contact you within 24 hours.
Our AI resolves most issues in under a minute. If your case needs human attention, it's automatically escalated and a support specialist will follow up via email within 24 hours. You can also click "Talk to a Human Agent" in the chat at any time.
